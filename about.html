About

Honest Vision

Kevin Roden is the name given to a wanderer of light and shadow — a quiet observer drawn to the fragile intersections of people, space, and time. His work captures the stillness between moments, the dialogue of light and form, and the quiet presence of everyday beauty.

Now based in Thailand, his photographs reflect a contemplative vision shaped by years of travel and observation. Guided by restraint and empathy, he seeks to reveal what remains when distractions fade — a moment of simple, honest clarity. He calls this pursuit Honest Vision — clarity in a world full of noise.

Behind this name stands a registered member of Royal Photographic Society of Thailand (RPST) , with a Member ID 7853.
